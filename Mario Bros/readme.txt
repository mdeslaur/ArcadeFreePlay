One of the annoyances of playing the Nintendo Mario Bros arcade game is
having to wait through the tutorial screens every single game.

The patches in these directories allow turning off the tutorial screens.

Dip Switch 4 has been repurposed from coin selection to enabling or
disabling the tutorial screens.

The IPS patches here support 4 different romsets:

mario  - The "Mario Bros. (US, revision E)" romset in MAME
mariof - The "Mario Bros. (US, revision F)" romset in MAME
mariog - The "Mario Bros. (US, revision G)" romset in MAME
braze  - The Braze Mario Bros High Score kit v1.3 rom

For original romsets, only the 7E rom needs to be patched and replaced. Use
a 2764. For the Braze kit, you will need a 27256 and a copy of the rom
image. You can download it from here:

https://www.brasington.org/arcade/download/

There are a few extra goodies in the "extras" directory, including MAME
patches to add support for the Braze High Score kit rom.
